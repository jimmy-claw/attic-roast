# What Fifty Beans' own label actually is

Transcribed 2026-09-16 from a photo Václav took of the back of one of their bags
(`IMG_20260916_191224171.jpg`), read with a Venice vision model (`qwen3-vl-235b-a22b`)
and cross-checked against my own pixel analysis of the same photo — the model said the
bag is teal `#20B2AA` (hue 180) and my independent cluster analysis of the file found
hue 190 earlier, so the read is real rather than invented.

## The important part first

**Their label is black type on a cream/off-white ground (`#F5F5F5`), on a teal bag.**

Our dark direction is ink ground with bone type — the *luminance inverse* of theirs. On a
shelf or a bag next to each other they read as two different families. That is almost
certainly what "does not match the existing ones at all" was about, and it applies to our
dark labels too, not only the colourful attempt I made.

## Layout

A light rectangular label on the bag, **two columns** separated by a thin vertical rule.

- **Full width, top:** `Pražená zrnková káva 100% arabica` (left) · `200 g` (right)
- **Full width, second row:** `PROFIL | ZELENÝ ČAJ, PECKOVICE, CITRUSY` · `balanced`
- **Left column:** the origin story (`O kávě:` — Chelchele, Gedebe, altitude, terroir);
  then `Metoda přípravy` — a full brew recipe: filter, water alkalinity 40–60 ppm,
  extraction 4–6 min, ratio 1:16, "best 14 days after roast"; then the producer block,
  a **QR code**, `Výrobce / DLY trading s.r.o., Drážní 253/7, 627 00 Brno`,
  `Praženo 18. 8. 2026`, `Minimální trvanlivost 18. 8. 2027`
- **Right column:** `Země: Etiopie` · `Závod: Chelchele` · `Zpracování: Promyté` ·
  `Odrůda: Heirloom` · `Nadmořská výška: 2000 m`; then **two taste sliders**, four squares
  each with one filled — `HOŘKÁ ↔ ŠŤAVNATÁ` (bitter ↔ juicy) and `KLIDNÁ ↔ ROZVERNÁ`
  (calm ↔ playful); then the black diamond logo with `Fiftybeans coffee`; vertical
  `fiftybeans.cz`; recycling mark `OTHER`; **EAN barcode `8 594226 860910`**

## Colours

| element | colour |
|---|---|
| bag | teal `#20B2AA` |
| label ground | cream / off-white `#F5F5F5` |
| type | black `#000000` |
| logo, filled slider squares | black |
| QR, barcode | black on white |

Essentially **two colours**: black and cream. No accent colour at all on the label.

## What this means for our design

1. **Our back label is redundant.** Theirs already carries the origin, the producer, the
   roast and best-before dates, a full brew recipe, a QR code and the barcode. Ours would
   restate a subset of that in a different voice. Recommended: do not print a back.
2. **If we want to match them**, the recipe is smaller than it looks: cream ground, black
   type, Czech, their field order, and their quietness — the mistake in my rejected
   "sunrise" attempt was not the light ground (that was right) but filling it with colour
   where they use none. A matching version is mostly *removing* things.
3. **The one genuine gap we could fill:** nothing on their label speaks to the room. A
   single line is the right size for that, and it is already written on the front.
